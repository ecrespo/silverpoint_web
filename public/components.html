UI component reference

The 17 form and display components silverpoint has shipped since 0.3 in React, Vue and Angular (and in Reflex through reflex-silverpoint-react), drawn with the charts' language: an exact frame, tone by hatching or by line weight, keyboard and screen-reader semantics from the native element. The 21 composed components and overlays of 0.7, and the slide deck of 0.9, are indexed at the end.

Composed components and overlays (0.7)

silverpoint 0.7 adds 21 more on the same subpaths, for 38 in all: SpSurface and SpGlyph, the building blocks, the composed components and the overlays. Each has a page with a live demo, its code in React, Vue, Angular and Reflex, its events and its props; Composed components shows them all on one page.

Slide decks (0.9)

silverpoint 0.9 adds SpDeck, a Marp/Marpit Markdown presentation drawn in silverpoint, on the deck subpath with @silverpoint/marp as its engine. This one is live (sp_deck): click into it and use the arrows; list items are revealed a step at a time, and the chart is a silverpoint fence. Slide decks has the full showcase, its code, directives, props and events.
